
Package 8 component-support contract
====================================

This addendum supersedes only the v3 capability statements for zero-valued
supports:

- Zero-valued fixed_all, rollers, symmetry boundaries, and true boundary-node
  pins are executable. Do not list "component_support" or "point_node_pin" as
  capability limits. Nonzero prescribed displacement remains capability-limited.
- A normal roller or symmetry condition constrains displacement normal to its
  named rectangle edge.
- roller_x means the constrained component ux=0 and roller_y means uy=0. These
  names never describe the direction in which motion is permitted.
- A pin must use selector.kind="boundary_point" and represents ux=uy=0 at one
  mesh node. Never turn it into a finite clamped segment.
- An unqualified named rectangle side, edge, or boundary denotes the complete
  edge for either a support or a load: use selector.kind="whole_edge". Words
  such as somewhere, center, near, tip, upper/lower, segment, portion, width,
  percentage, between, or corner qualify the extent and must retain the
  corresponding partial or finite selector instead.
- Bare directional placement without a boundary noun, such as “the load on the
  right is 10,” identifies selector.edge="right" but not its extent; use
  selector.kind="unspecified_extent".
- A capability-limited applied moment with a compound unit such as N mm does not
  justify proposing a global N-mm-MPa unit system. Retain the stated moment unit
  and ask only for facts relevant to its location or explicit reformulation.
