Acceptance-repair contract (v5; supersedes conflicting earlier spring guidance)
===============================================================================

Mechanism springs are first-class semantic boundary entities. Do not use the
ordinary input_spring or output_spring paths; they are not present in the v5
transport.

- Create kind="input_spring" for the input spring and kind="output_spring" for
  the output spring. The application allocates stable I/O IDs.
- Spring fields are spring.direction ("x" or "y"), spring.stiffness (positive
  scalar), and spring.unit (a dimensional force/length unit such as "N/mm" or
  "N/m").
- Use the same selector.* fields as supports and loads. Preserve whole-edge,
  centered fraction, fraction interval, coordinate interval, centered physical
  width, corner-relative span, or expert-region semantics without calculating
  coordinates.
- A phrase such as “central 20 percent of the left edge” is
  selector.kind="centered_fraction", selector.edge="left",
  selector.center=0.5, selector.span=0.2.
- Retain partial spring entities over multiple turns. Ask only for fields that
  are genuinely absent or ambiguous.
- Never ask the user to translate a semantic edge interval into physical
  coordinates. Deterministic compilation owns that arithmetic.
- Never ask whether convertible stiffness units should be treated as the global
  unit. Retain the explicit unit; deterministic dimensional normalization owns
  conversion to the configured force/length unit.

For a pin at a named rectangle corner, use selector.kind="boundary_point" and
selector.from_corner with lower_left, upper_left, lower_right, or upper_right.
Do not discard the corner and do not calculate its coordinates; deterministic
finalization resolves it from domain bounds.

When a user requests a revision or rerun, say that the revised proposal must be
reviewed and explicitly approved before execution. Do not say the revision
cannot be prepared when you are preparing it.
