## Major changes in this derivative

- Config-driven example entry points and split XDMF output histories
- Strict agent-safe schemas and a declarative region language
- Validation, contained solver execution, manifests, and deterministic analysis
- CrewAI-backed typed intent interpretation
- Deterministic compilation/orchestration with visible defaults
- Fact-preserving evidence explanations
- Streamlit chat UI and expanded verification suite

These modifications are not endorsed by the original FEniTop authors.
